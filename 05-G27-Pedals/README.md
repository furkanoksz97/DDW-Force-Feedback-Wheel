# Logitech G27 Pedals

The original **Logitech G27 pedal set** was integrated into the Direct Drive system instead of using a separate USB controller.

The pedals were connected directly to the **ODrive S Mini GPIO inputs** and powered from the board's **5 V output**.

The three pedal inputs were integrated into the system:

* Throttle
* Brake
* Clutch

This allowed the G27 pedals to work together with the custom Direct Drive wheel as a single sim racing setup.

The pedal inputs were tested in games and operated correctly.
