\# BLDC Motor



The project started with a hoverboard BLDC motor.



The hoverboard motor was selected as the main motor for the Direct Drive Force Feedback system. The motor was later integrated with the ODrive S Mini motor controller.



\## Motor Selection



A hoverboard BLDC motor was preferred because its large diameter and direct-drive structure are suitable for producing high torque at relatively low rotational speeds.



\## Next Step



The next stage of the project was integrating the motor with the ODrive S Mini.
